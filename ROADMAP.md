# Roadmap

Minecraft-style themed version roadmap. The scope is staged so the reader and live stage ship before broader discovery and language support.

## v0.1.0-alpha - The Stage Update (current MVP)
- Home page with discovered books, chapters, and verse list.
- Left-sidebar multi-shloka tabs (`Ctrl+1` through `Ctrl+9`).
- English chapter loader for `content/bg/bg-10.en.md`.
- Built-in and customizable declarative views.
- Interactive block placement, visibility, and dynamic reflow.
- Minimal Dark, True Black, and Warm Light themes.

## v0.2.0-alpha - The Search & Navigation Update
- Global instant search (`Ctrl+K`) and verse jump palette.
- Persistent session state.

## v0.3.0-alpha - The Multilingual Update
- Hindi (`*.hi.md`) support and instant EN / HI switching.

## v0.4.0-beta - The Grand Grantha Update
- Multi-canto support for Srimad Bhagavatam, Chaitanya Charitamrita, and Bhakti Rasamrita Sindhu.

## v1.0.0 - The Native GPUI Update
- Rust + GPUI native desktop shell reusing the exact `content/` and `appearance/` contracts.
